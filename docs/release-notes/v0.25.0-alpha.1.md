## EML v0.25.0-alpha.1 (Experimental)

Asks and check-backs are separate: whose move a task is and when an agent looks at it again no longer change each other. Early builds: things may break. Offered only to cockpits on the Alpha (experimental) update channel.

**Brain schema 25.** The first start migrates every brain to schema 25, with a backup first: a task's schedule moves out of its delegation into a field of its own, and a wake-up names who it is for. A cockpit on schema 24 stops syncing a shared space once a member writes from 25: every member of a shared space moves to this build. Going back to v0.24 needs the backup from before the update.

### What's new

- **Your move keeps the agent's check-back:** an agent can hand you a step (sign, call, send) and still look again at its time; handing over or reviewing never clears a wake-up any more.
- **end_turn shows the result:** after every turn the agent reads what the task now holds — its asks, its check-back, repeating times and next run — in your local time.
- **Done wakes the agent:** "Your move" items in the Inbox have Done, which tells the agent at once; asks can carry a due date.
- **Reminders for yourself:** a wake-up can be for you instead of the agent; at its time the task lands in your Inbox as your move.
- **Looks good closes a check-back task:** on a task with a wake-up and no repeating times, Looks good closes it and ends the agent's check; the task panel shows who checks back, with Reschedule and Remove.

### Details

- end_turn takes `ask` ({who, what, kind: do | review, due}) and `check` ({when, why} or "none"). Leaving `check` out keeps the task's wake-up. Sessions started before still send `next`, which is read as the ask and the check it stands for.
- The schedule tool takes `by`: leave it out for the agent, name the user for a reminder.
- Scheduling a task no longer delegates it to the space's agent; taking a task back from its agent leaves its schedule alone.
- Every schedule change writes a journal line with its cause: a turn's end, the schedule tool, Looks good or Close, a wake-up that fired, the owner's Remove or Reschedule.
